Privacy Policy

TechRecycle uses this website to publish repair education, recycling information, and business enquiry forms for companies that want help with laptop recycling, data destruction, and responsible IT equipment disposal. This policy explains what information may be collected and how it is used.

Information submitted through forms

When you submit an enquiry, we may receive your name, company name, email address, telephone number, Johannesburg area, estimated equipment quantity, data status, and the message you choose to send. We use this information only to respond to the enquiry, understand the type of recycling or data destruction support requested, and keep basic business communication records.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The website may use normal browser technologies such as cookies, server logs, analytics tools, and advertising scripts when enabled. These technologies may help us understand which pages are useful, improve navigation, prevent spam, and show advertising in the future. If Google AdSense is enabled later, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads based on visits to this and other websites.

Advertising transparency: TechRecycle may use responsible display advertising. Ads should not change the editorial position of our repair and recycling content.

How information is protected

We aim to keep enquiry information limited, relevant, and business-focused. Do not send passwords, recovery keys, private documents, device login details, or confidential files through the public form. If data destruction is required, discuss the data status first and arrange the correct handling method before equipment changes hands.

Third-party links

TechRecycle may link to official manufacturer support pages, standards bodies, environmental resources, and repair-related references. External sites have their own privacy practices and policies.

Your choices

You can contact TechRecycle to ask about an enquiry you submitted, request correction of your contact details, or ask for your business enquiry details to be removed where we no longer need them for legitimate communication.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@techrecycle.co.za.
