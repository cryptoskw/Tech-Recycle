What this guide explains

A refurbisher checklist for testing battery, screen, keyboard, ports, storage, thermals, performance, and cosmetic grade before resale. This page is written to support practical repair decisions without unsafe shortcuts or fake file promises.

How technicians approach it

Start with symptom confirmation, model identification, board inspection, and safe isolation tests. Record what changes and what does not change before replacing parts.

What to document

Write down the device model, board number, visible damage, test results, customer concern, and final recommendation so the repair can be reviewed later.

Common mistakes

Do not guess parts, do not flash unknown firmware, do not skip backups, and do not ignore data-risk communication.

Next repair decision

Use the checklist below to decide whether the machine needs software work, parts replacement, board diagnosis, or responsible recycling.

Technician workflow

  1. Confirm the exact symptom and reproduce it before opening the device.
  2. Record the brand, full model, board number, storage type, charger type, and visible condition.
  3. Remove simple variables first: charger, battery where possible, external devices, SSD, RAM, display, keyboard, or daughterboards depending on the fault.
  4. Take measurements or software test results before replacing parts so the decision is based on evidence.
  5. Explain data risk to the owner before firmware work, storage work, or board-level repair.
  6. Document the final result, what was changed, and what still needs future monitoring.

Warnings before you continue

  • Do not use repair information for stolen-device unlocking, password bypass, or unauthorized access.
  • Do not flash firmware without a verified backup and exact compatibility checks.
  • When the device contains customer data, explain risk clearly before storage, firmware, or board work.

Repair bench checklist

  • Device model recorded
  • Board inspected
  • Symptoms confirmed
  • Data risk explained
  • Repair path chosen
  • Final notes saved
