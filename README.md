- Made cards softer and less boxy with refined spacing and rounded sections.
- Added external stock image URLs across hero, cards, silo pages, and article pages.

- Added safe external authority links to manufacturer and standards pages where relevant.
- Updated meta descriptions, Open Graph images, schema, sitemap, favicon, and cache busting to v5.
