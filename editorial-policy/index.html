Editorial Policy

How TechRecycle creates, updates, and protects the quality of its repair and recycling content.

Updated July 2026

Editorial purpose

TechRecycle publishes practical repair and recycling information for technicians, refurbishers, office managers, IT departments, and businesses that need responsible equipment disposal. The aim is to make laptop repair, data destruction, and recycling decisions easier to understand.

Original content standard

Articles are written to be useful, specific, and relevant to the page topic. We avoid copied competitor text, doorway pages, misleading download promises, fake testimonials, and pages that exist only to rank without helping the reader.

Safety and legal boundaries

TechRecycle does not publish instructions for stolen-device unlocking, BIOS password bypass, cracked software, illegal firmware downloads, or pirated schematics. Firmware, boardview, and schematic topics are handled as education and safe diagnostic guidance.

External references

Where useful, articles may link to official manufacturer support pages, environmental regulations, data protection resources, and recognised technical guidance. External links are included to help users verify important details and do not automatically mean TechRecycle endorses every statement on those sites.

Advertising and affiliate content

TechRecycle may later use display ads or affiliate links for tools and repair equipment. Advertising should not decide the editorial conclusion of an article. If paid or affiliate relationships are added in the future, the site should keep clear disclosure where relevant.

Corrections

If you see outdated, incomplete, or unclear information, contact info@techrecycle.co.za with the page URL and the issue.

Helpful content

Pages must answer real search intent and avoid thin filler.

Safe repair

Repair guidance must respect ownership, safety, data, and compatibility.

Business clarity

Recycling pages must clearly say TechRecycle is not a buy-back service.
