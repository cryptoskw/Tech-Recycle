About TechRecycle Repair Library

TechRecycle is a technician-focused repair knowledge library for laptop diagnostics, BIOS education, board notes, tools, refurbishment, and recycling.

Built for repair technicians and refurbishers

TechRecycle Repair Library exists to organize practical laptop repair education into clear silos: BIOS and firmware, schematics, BoardView tools, repair techniques, common laptop faults, parts, recycling, and technician notes.

The site is not a file dump and does not promote illegal firmware downloads, password bypass, or stolen-device unlocking. The goal is safer repair thinking, better documentation, and responsible reuse.
